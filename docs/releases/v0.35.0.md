# 📦 Velio - Release v0.35.0

---

> **🧭 Summary**
>
> First public release of **Velio**, a cycling route planner built on open data for Spain, Portugal and Andorra, live at **[velio.cub1z.es](https://velio.cub1z.es)**. Tell it how far and how much climbing: it draws a loop back to your door, or a route through your stops, and scores it before you ride, with the surface kilometre by kilometre, the traffic, the climbing and any road ridden twice. This release covers the whole product: the routing and scoring engine, the web app, accounts, and the production deploy with daily encrypted backups.

---

## 📄 Release Notes

### 🔁 Loops cut to your distance

- **Distance, climbing and bike**: choose how far, how much climbing (flat, rolling or hilly) and which bike (road, city, gravel or mountain); the engine adjusts what counts as a good surface for that bike. With location permission the loop starts where you are, or anywhere on the map.
- **An engine that corrects itself**: it places points on a ring around the start, routes them with Valhalla and rescales until the loop matches the distance and climbing asked for. A stretch that rides the same road twice is redrawn.
- **Up to four loops to pick from**: ask for up to three alternatives; results arrive one by one as the engine finds them.

### 📍 Point-to-point routes

- **From A to B through your stops**: up to 20 stops, with up to two scored alternatives to compare.

### 📊 A score before you ride

- **Cycling score out of 10**: blends distance accuracy, traffic, surface for the chosen bike, climbing and repeated road.
- **Surface kilometre by kilometre**: tarmac, gravel and trails along the route, as a bar you can read at a glance.
- **Real elevation**: profile, gain and grades from the Copernicus DEM GLO-30 at 30 m, with a 0.62 m mean error against Valhalla's own readings on a 74 km mountain route; maximum grade and climb load included.

### 🔍 Analyse any ride

- **Upload a GPX or paste a polyline**: get the same score, surface, traffic and elevation for a route you already have.

### 💾 Saved routes and export

- **Your routes in one place**: save, rename, search, filter loops and point-to-point routes, and delete one or many at once.
- **Ready for the GPS**: export any route as GPX with per-point elevation (Garmin, Wahoo, Komoot) or as GeoJSON.

### 🗺️ Maps

- **Map, satellite and relief**: vector map in light and dark, satellite imagery and optional relief shading, all without API keys.

### 👤 Accounts

- **Email or Google**: sign up with an email and password confirmed by a six-digit code, or continue with Google.
- **Account settings**: change your name and password, see and close open sessions, and delete your account with an emailed code, which erases your data.

### 🌍 Coverage

- **Spain, Portugal and Andorra**, the Canary Islands, the Azores and Madeira included. A point outside them gets a clear answer instead of a routing error, and riders can **ask for their country**: requests are counted so the next region follows demand.

### 🌐 Languages and devices

- **English and Spanish**: English at the root and Spanish under `/es`; the URL decides the language.
- **Phones first**: every page is laid out for small screens, with the planner form in a sheet over a full-screen map.
- **Public pages load complete**: the landing, the privacy policy and the terms are prerendered, so they paint with their final content and font.

### ⚖️ Privacy and terms

- **Privacy policy and terms of service** at `/privacy` and `/terms`, linked from the footer and from the sign-up consent. Velio has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

---

## 🏗️ Under the hood

- **Backend**: Python 3.12, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy (async) and Alembic over Postgres 16, Redis 7 for rate limiting, and Valhalla 3.8 for routing over one graph built from the Geofabrik extracts.
- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, HeroUI v3 and MapLibre GL.
- **Delivery**: one repository with `backend/` and `frontend/`, CI per side on GitHub Actions and images on Docker Hub. A GitHub release deploys on Dokploy, waits for `/api/health` to report its version and runs a smoke test.
- **Backups**: the database is dumped every day, encrypted with GPG and stored in Backblaze B2 (EU) for 14 days; one command restores it.

### 🏷️ Version

- Backend and API contract at **0.35.0**, the first published release.
