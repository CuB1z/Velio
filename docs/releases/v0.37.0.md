# 📦 Velio - Release v0.37.0

---

> **🧭 Summary**
>
> Minor release **0.37.0** of **Velio**. On a phone, Velio now behaves like an installed app: dialogs and menus are sheets that slide up from the bottom, saved routes keep their actions one tap away instead of in rows of buttons, and the planner sheet follows your finger and settles like an iOS sheet. CI and the image builds moved to Node 24 actions on a pinned Ubuntu image.

---

## 📄 Release Notes

### ✨ Features

- **Sheets instead of floating dialogs on phones**: saving a route, renaming or deleting one, deleting the account and asking for your country now slide up from the bottom with a handle, close by dragging them down, tapping outside or with Escape, and keep their buttons full width within reach of the thumb. From tablet width up they stay centred dialogs.
- **Route actions where you need them**: every saved route has its own "⋯" menu (rename, export GPX, export GeoJSON, select, delete), which also opens with a long press or a right click, so a route no longer has to be opened to rename or export it. On phones the list keeps the search and a "+" in one row, with selection and page size in a second "⋯", and selecting routes turns the header into a count with one full-width delete button at the bottom.
- **A tidier route page**: the header fits one line (back, name, menu); Export stays next to the menu on larger screens. On phones the figures rise over the map as a sheet with rounded corners.
- **A planner sheet that moves like the phone's own**: it follows the finger frame by frame, answers a quick flick even when short, stretches with resistance past its ends, can be dragged from the result, and settles with the same spring as an iOS sheet. Dialogs and menus use the same motion, and all of it stops when the phone asks for reduced motion.

### 🐛 Fixes

- The status bar takes the colour of the theme chosen in Velio, not only the phone's.
- No more grey flash on taps or buttons that stay highlighted after a tap, and pulling down inside the app no longer reloads the page and loses the route.
- A menu reopens on the first tap right after it was closed.
- In selection mode the tick no longer covers the route type.

### 🧹 Maintenance

- **CI on Node 24**: every GitHub Action moved to its current major (checkout, setup-python, setup-node, pnpm, Docker build and login), and every job runs on a pinned `ubuntu-26.04` image instead of `ubuntu-latest`.
- The frontend keeps only components under `components/`; hooks, helpers, static data and the map types moved to `hooks/`, `lib/`, `constants/` and `types/`.

### 🏷️ Version

- Backend and API contract bumped to **0.37.0**.
